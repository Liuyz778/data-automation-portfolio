## Title（70 字符内）
Python Data Automation | Web Scraping · Excel Reports · Dashboards

## Overview

Spending hours each week copying data, fixing spreadsheets or rebuilding the same report? I automate that.

I build small, reliable Python tools for business owners and analysts:

• **Web scraping** – product listings, directories, job boards, real-estate portals → clean Excel/CSV/Google Sheets. Pagination, retries and rate-limiting handled.
• **Excel / CSV automation** – clean messy exports (mixed dates, text prices, duplicates), merge files, generate formatted multi-sheet reports with charts in one click.
• **Interactive dashboards** – Streamlit + Plotly dashboards with filters, KPIs and live data from files, Google Sheets or SQL. Deployed free or on your server.

How I work:
1. You send a sample and describe the result you want
2. I reply within hours with a fixed price and timeline
3. You get working code, documentation and a short walkthrough video
4. Unlimited fixes until it does exactly what you asked

Tech: Python, pandas, openpyxl, requests/BeautifulSoup/Playwright, Streamlit, Plotly, SQL, Google Sheets API.

Portfolio samples on request (web scraper · automated Excel sales report · interactive dashboard). Based in Malaysia (GMT+8), available 7 days a week.

## 投标（Proposal）模板

规则：
- 前两句必须针对他的需求，证明你读了帖子。模板句一眼就能看出来，会被直接跳过。
- 短。6–10 句，不超过 150 词。
- 结尾提一个具体问题，让他回复。
- 附上最相关的一个作品链接，不要全贴。

### 模板 A · 爬虫类

Hi [Name],

You need [exact thing from the post, e.g. "all 2,000 supplier listings from X with name, phone and email"] in Excel. I've built scrapers for very similar directory sites, including handling pagination and the rate limits X uses.

Plan: I'll pull [fields] from [site], de-duplicate, and deliver an Excel + CSV within [2] days. If you want to re-run it later I'll include the script with a one-line command.

Here's a similar scraper I built (1,000 items, 50 pages, with retries): [portfolio link]

Quick question: do you need the data once, or refreshed on a schedule?

[Your name]

## 筛选哪些单子值得投

投：
- 预算 ≥ $50，描述具体，附了样本文件或网址
- 客户有付款验证（Payment verified）且历史评分 ≥ 4.5
- 发布 24 小时内，投标人数 < 20

不投：
- "需要 10 年经验的 Senior 数据工程师" 这种大活
- 预算 $5–10 的
- 要求抓取需要登录的社交平台个人数据（法律风险）
- 描述一句话、没有任何细节的
